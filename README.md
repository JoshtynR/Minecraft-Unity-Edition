# Minecraft Unity Edition

A Minecraft-inspired sandbox built in Unity, bringing the terrain and atmosphere of Beta 1.7.3 together with a growing collection of survival features and quality-of-life improvements. 

a fork of this repo https://github.com/BossSloth/Minecraft-Unity

Explore sprawling landscapes, build a home, gather resources, craft equipment, and travel into the Nether. World generation follows Beta 1.7.3, including its biomes, caves, trees, ores, and decorations.

### Features

- **Classic world generation:** Seed-based Beta-style terrain, snowy biomes, caves, and biome-coloured grass and leaves.
- **Survival gameplay:** Health, hunger, eating, sprinting, fall damage, armour, and tool durability.
- **Crafting and storage:** Crafting tables, working furnaces, single and double chests, and persistent inventories.
- **Living worlds:** Passive animals, hostile monsters, breeding, farming, and growing crops.
- **Interactive blocks:** Doors, trapdoors, beds, falling sand and gravel, and leaf decay.
- **Fluids and dimensions:** Flowing water and lava, fluid reactions, functional buckets, and Nether portal travel.
- **Classic atmosphere:** Day/night cycles, sunsets, sun and moon, 3D clouds, fog, smooth lighting, and ambient occlusion.
- **Player presentation:** First-person hands and held items, movement animations, equipment previews, sounds, and separate menu/world music.
- **Building convenience:** An optional TooManyItems-style item browser alongside the survival inventory.

### A classic foundation, with room to grow

This isn’t a strict recreation of one Minecraft version. Beta 1.7.3 provides the foundation for world generation, while selected newer features—such as hunger and modern chest models—expand the experience.

The project is actively developed. Expect unfinished features, occasional bugs, and changes between updates. Back up important worlds before updating.
